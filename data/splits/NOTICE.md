# Third-party data notice

The files in this directory (`train.jsonl`, `val.jsonl`, `frozen_test.jsonl`) redistribute text from the
public datasets below, after cleaning (CRLF to LF, whitespace strip), duplicate removal and relabeling to a
binary `label` (0 = benign, 1 = injection). Each record's `source` and `source_label` fields name its origin
and its original label. Records with `source: benign_hard` are synthetic and were generated by
`training/benign_hard.py` in this repository.
